## The first flight
Five agents (**requirements, architect, dev, review, bugfix**) driven by a handful of skills: `/plan`, `/implement`, `/progress`, `/fix`, `/review` and `/update-system`.

Each ticket gets its own artifact folder with requirements, architecture, data entities, slices, progress and review. Work stops for a human yes after every phase.

## What it taught me
- Agents do much better with **written artifacts** than with chat history.
- **Approval gates** don't slow you down. They stop work from going wrong quietly.
- A harness needs a **console**. That lesson became Mothership.
