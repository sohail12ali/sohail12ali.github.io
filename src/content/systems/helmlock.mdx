## Why a v3
Mothership proved the ideas and showed where the weight was. Too much lived in the core. Helmlock keeps the gates and the traceability and moves everything else out into plugins.

## Architecture
- **The kernel** covers four things and nothing more: a context tree, a service registry, scoped effects and events.
- **Plugins** add the rest: the CLI, agent packs, runtimes, voice, remote channels (such as Telegram), ticket workflow and views.
- **Two repos.** A *delivery* repo holds skills, agents and the console. A *knowledge* repo holds the vault. The machinery can change without touching what you know.
- **Three layers of configuration:** system → workspace → project. Each layer has its own `CLAUDE.md`, skills and agents.
- **Files only.** Markdown for prose, TOML for structure, JSONL for history.

## Designing in public
Helmlock is planned with its own `/plan` skill. It turns blueprints, decisions, flows and mockups into one interactive plan page. The design is the first artifact the system produces.
