## The question
How do you build a friendly spoken companion that answers new employees' questions and never says anything that isn't in the approved material?

## What I explored
I wrote up eighteen techniques, each with its trade-offs, and tested them against a shared eval set. They include:

- **Citation forcing**: no source, no sentence.
- **An NLI verifier**: every claim is checked for entailment against retrieved passages.
- **Constrained decoding**: the model can only phrase what retrieval returned.
- **RAFT-style fine-tuning**, which trains the model to ignore distractor documents.
- **A semantic cache** of approved answers for the questions everyone asks.
- **Recursive LMs** for long, layered source material.

## Takeaway
No single technique is enough. Grounding works as layered defenses, the same way approval gates do in an agent harness.
